Talabat ElBeit: three design directions

All three use the same new layout. Only the look changes: colors, type and shapes. Each comes in light and dark, and each works in Arabic, right to left.

Left phone: the shopping list in English, light theme. Right phone: adding an item in Arabic, dark theme. The changes shared by all three are listed at the bottom.

1 Daftar, the fridge notebook My pick

The list looks like the paper list on the fridge: ruled lines, blue ballpoint ink, a red margin line, and the family name in Ruqaa, the script most Egyptians handwrite in. Rows are lines, not cards, so a long list stays calm. "Due today" gets a highlighter stroke.

Why I'd pick it: it's the most personal of the three, it fits what the app replaces, and lines take less height than cards, so more items fit on screen. The margin line moves to the right side in Arabic on its own.

What changes in every direction

These layout and behavior changes come with whichever look you pick.

More room for the list

The add form fills half the screen today. It becomes a slim bar at the bottom, and tapping it opens a sheet.

Faster adding

A quantity stepper and unit chips replace the dropdown. Due date has shortcuts: No rush, Today, Tomorrow, Pick a time.

No instant "overdue"

The due date becomes optional. Today it defaults to "now", so every new item turns red a minute after you add it.

To buy, then Bought

Bought items move to their own section at the bottom, with a Clear button to tidy up after a shop.

Safer deleting

Swipe a row to delete it, with an Undo bar for a few seconds. Today a red bin button on every row deletes on one tap.

Family sheet

Tap the member faces in the header to see members, switch language, leave or delete the family, and sign out. This replaces the side drawer.

Real icons

Ionicons, already in the project, replace emoji, so icons look the same on every phone.

Arabic and English

Full translation, right-to-left layout, Arabic-Indic digits (٢ كجم) and Arabic dates. Units read naturally: باكو, كرتونة.

Sign-in and family screens

Same look, with a two-tab switch (Sign in / Create account, Join / Create family) instead of stacked buttons.

One theme system

One file of color, spacing and type tokens, read through a useTheme() hook. The two duplicate color files go away.
